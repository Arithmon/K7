# Falsifier ledger

A falsifier is attached to the **claim layer it actually tests**. Failure of one physical identification is not automatically promoted to failure of the topology, and failure of an analytic construction is not automatically promoted to experimental refutation of every arithmetic relation.

## F1 — compact analytic completion

**Claim layer:** compact-global K7 analytic construction.

**Current status:** open / conditional.

**Failure mode:** the required compact topology/datum wrappers or anisotropic perturbation theorem cannot be established for the chosen construction, or the candidate compact datum is shown to violate a necessary hypothesis.

**What it falsifies:** the current compact-global construction route.

**What it does not by itself falsify:** every algebraic relation in the historical ledger.

## F2 — `theta_23` octant

**Claim layer:** frozen K7 PMNS identification.

**K7 value:** the historical K7 relation lies in the upper octant.

**Current situation:** tension exists with fits whose best fit is in the lower octant; the octant is not yet treated here as decisively settled.

**Refutation rule:** a future oscillation combination establishes the lower octant with a predeclared significance threshold incompatible with the frozen K7 value.

**Prediction-status note:** this is a prospective test of a frozen historical retrodiction, not evidence that the original formula was target-blind.

## F3 — `delta_CP = 197°`

**Claim layer:** frozen K7 CP-phase identification.

**Refutation rule:** a future high-precision oscillation result excludes 197° under the declared convention and confidence rule.

**Prediction-status note:** the value is treated as a locked retrodiction under prospective validation, not as an a-priori discovery prediction.

## F4 — `K7-P1` Higgs trilinear benchmark

**Claim layer:** minimal single-doublet Higgs identification built on the frozen K7 `lambda_H`.

**Arithmetic benchmark:**

`lambda_H = sqrt(17)/32`

`rho3_tree = g_hhh(tree)/v = 6 lambda_H = 3 sqrt(17)/16`

**Refutation rule:** only meaningful after the renormalization scheme, scale, loop matching and experimental pseudo-observable are frozen. A future measurement excluding the resulting frozen physical prediction falsifies this **minimal Higgs identification**.

**It does not automatically falsify:** the K7 topology. A demonstrated higher-dimensional Higgs operator could instead falsify the minimal operator basis.

## F5 — clean-room reproducibility

**Claim layer:** computational evidence.

**Refutation rule:** an independent clean clone cannot reproduce a claimed cell from public inputs and stated commands.

**What it falsifies:** that cell's reproducibility claim until repaired.

This is a real falsifier in OpenWave: a number that exists only in a private workspace is not yet a reproducible column result.
