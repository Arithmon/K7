The exhaustive configuration scan is evidence about model selection; it does not make the selection disappear.

## 2. Continuously adjustable parameters

The Type-I algebraic layer is currently presented as having **no continuously adjustable parameter after its ledger is frozen**.

That is a narrower claim than “zero free parameters” under OpenWave, because OpenWave also counts discrete and sector-specific choices.

## 3. Historical numerical relations

The following class is treated conservatively:

- electroweak relations such as `sin²θ_W = 3/13`;
- `lambda_H = sqrt(17)/32`;
- charged-lepton relations including Koide and mass ratios;
- PMNS angle relations;
- `delta_CP = 197°`;
- other Type-I numerical agreements developed while the corresponding experimental targets were already known.

For OpenWave purposes these are **historical retrodictions / calibration-sensitive discoveries unless a relation has a documented target-blind origin**.

A later freeze can make future comparisons prospective, but cannot retroactively change the discovery status.
