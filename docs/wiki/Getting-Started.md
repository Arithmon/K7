# Getting Started

## What's your background?

Choose the path that fits you best:

### I'm curious about science (no math required)
Start with **[K₇ for Everyone](GIFT-for-Everyone.html)**: a complete guide using everyday analogies. Then check the **[FAQ](FAQ.html)** for common questions.

### I'm a physicist
Read **[For Physicists](For-Physicists.html)** for a concise technical overview: the E₈×E₈ and G₂ inputs, the dictionary, and the 33 relations with their status. Then dive into the **[Main Paper](Paper-Main-Framework.html)**.

### I'm a geometer / mathematician
Read **[For Geometers](For-Geometers.html)** for the computational pipeline: TCS construction, PINN metrics, spectral extraction, and Lean 4 bridges. The **[Explicit G₂ Metric](Paper-Explicit-G2-Metric.html)** paper is your entry point.

### I'm interested in formal verification
Read **[For Formalization Experts](For-Formalization-Experts.html)** for the Lean 4 architecture: 156 .lean files, 14 axioms (4 imported from the literature, 10 interval certificates), no `sorry`; the 33 relations are collected in `relations_v36_certified`. See **[Lean Formalization](Lean-Formalization.html)** for the full picture.

### I want to check the numbers
Go straight to **[Observable Reference](Observable-Reference.html)** (the 33 relations with formulas, data and status), the **[Validation Summary](Validation-Summary.html)** (the five classes and the decision rule) and **[Statistical Evidence](Statistical-Evidence.html)** (the look-elsewhere test: closeness to the data does not distinguish K₇).

### I want to cite K₇
See **[Citation Guide](Citation-Guide.html)** for BibTeX, APA, and DOI information.

## Essential Reading Order

1. **[K₇ for Everyone](GIFT-for-Everyone.html)** or **[For Physicists](For-Physicists.html)** (depending on background)
2. **[Main Framework Paper](Paper-Main-Framework.html)**: the correspondence, its dictionary, its residue and its decision rule
3. **[Observable Reference](Observable-Reference.html)**, the 33 relations in one table
4. **[Statistical Evidence](Statistical-Evidence.html)**, how distinctive the agreement is
5. **[Lean Formalization](Lean-Formalization.html)**, machine-checked arithmetic
