## The hypothesis

The dimensionless constants of the Standard Model are arithmetic and topological invariants of a compact manifold with G₂ holonomy, not free parameters to be fitted. The specific geometry has Betti numbers (b₂, b₃) = (21, 77) and an E₈×E₈ motivated architecture.

One relation shows what that means in a single line. The number of chiral fermion generations follows from the topological constraint (rank(E₈) + N_gen) × b₂ = N_gen × b₃, that is (8 + N_gen) × 21 = N_gen × 77, whose only integer solution is N_gen = 3. Not declared. Derived.

## What is not being claimed

Only the thirty-three parameter-free relations are direct consequences of the frozen ledger. The remaining observables are separated into declared categories: one-step extractions that use a named experimental anchor, multi-step chains conditional on the mechanism invoked, and structural diagnostics with no direct comparison. Counting all ninety-five as independent predictions would be the easiest available way to oversell this work, and the framework's own tables refuse to do it.

Numerical precision is reported as a secondary figure. A framework of this kind can always be made to look impressive by leading with sub-percent agreement, which is exactly what a well-tuned fit also produces. The claim is structural, not numerical.

## Why this is not numerology, as a commitment rather than an argument

The ledger, the formula grammar and the target list were frozen and deposited before any relation was searched for. The statistical procedure was fixed in advance too, and it is deliberately not bespoke: it is calibrated against historical verdicts, so that Eddington's fine-structure argument fails it and the quantum Hall relation passes it. A test only its author's framework can pass is not a test.

Under that pre-registered null battery, the honest outcome is modest and is reported as modest: one relation, m_H/m_W, survives at exact rank-one uniqueness, with Koide's Q = 2/3 close behind. Earlier versions of this work led with joint coincidence probabilities around 10⁻³⁴⁶. Those figures are real, they are retained as internal diagnostics, and they have been deliberately removed from the headline, because they measure something other than what a skeptical reader needs to know.

## What is open

Three questions are load-bearing and unresolved, and they are stated at the same prominence as the results rather than buried. Does the compact geometry exist? The global-analytic layer remains conditional on a declared hypothesis pack. Where does the gauge sector come from? The branching chain is compatible with Standard Model matter content but is not yet a physical breaking mechanism, and this is the largest gap in the work. Why (21, 77)? Five routes to deriving the Betti numbers were investigated and closed; one explicit residual remains, b₂ + b₃ = 98 = dim(K₇) · dim(G₂).

## The question

Most proposals that attempt to explain the constants of Nature eventually fail. Perhaps this one will too.

But the question does not go away with them. If the constants are not fundamental, they come from somewhere. This is one attempt to answer that while making every assumption explicit, every calculation reproducible, every prediction public, and every route to refutation available in advance.

Whether the hypothesis survives is now a matter for mathematics and experiment, not for authority.
