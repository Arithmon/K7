{\small

One compact seven-dimensional geometry. Zero adjustable parameters.
Thirty-three exact algebraic relations among Standard Model constants,
machine-checked in Lean~4 with no unproven step, from a ledger of inputs
frozen and publicly deposited before the search began.

And one date. If DUNE measures the neutrino CP phase $\delta_{\CP}$
outside $[182°, 212°]$ at $3\sigma$, this framework is wrong, nothing in
it can be tuned to survive, and the prediction on record is $197°$. First
beam is targeted for 2031.

The second paragraph is what earns the first. A framework with no free
parameters cannot absorb a bad measurement. That is the cost of the
claim, and it is the reason the claim is worth checking rather than
dismissing.

\vspace{0.35em}
\noindent\textbf{The hypothesis.}\enspace The dimensionless constants of
the Standard Model are arithmetic and topological invariants of a compact
manifold with $\Gtwo$ holonomy, not free parameters to be fitted. The
specific geometry has Betti numbers $(\btwo, \bthree) = (21, 77)$ and an
$\E_8 \times \E_8$ motivated architecture. One relation shows what that
means in a single line: the number of chiral fermion generations follows
from the topological constraint
$(\rank(\E_8) + N_{\rm gen}) \times \btwo = N_{\rm gen} \times \bthree$,
that is $(8 + N_{\rm gen}) \times 21 = N_{\rm gen} \times 77$, whose only
integer solution is $N_{\rm gen} = 3$. Not declared. Derived.

\noindent\textbf{What is not being claimed.}\enspace Only the
thirty-three parameter-free relations are direct consequences of the
frozen ledger. The remaining observables are separated into declared
categories: one-step extractions that use a named experimental anchor,
multi-step chains conditional on the mechanism invoked, and structural
diagnostics with no direct comparison. Counting all ninety-five as
independent predictions would be the easiest available way to oversell
this work, and the framework's own tables refuse to do it. Numerical
precision is reported as a secondary figure: sub-percent agreement is
exactly what a well-tuned fit also produces. The claim is structural, not
numerical.

\vspace{0.35em}
\noindent\textbf{Why this is not numerology, as a commitment rather than
an argument.}\enspace The ledger, the formula grammar and the target list
were frozen and deposited before any relation was searched for. The
statistical procedure was fixed in advance too, and it is deliberately
not bespoke: it is calibrated against historical verdicts, so that
Eddington's fine-structure argument fails it and the quantum Hall
relation passes it. A test only its author's framework can pass is not a
test. Under that pre-registered null battery the honest outcome is modest
and is reported as modest: one relation, $m_H/m_W$, survives at exact
rank-one uniqueness, with Koide's $Q = 2/3$ close behind. Earlier
versions led with joint coincidence probabilities around $10^{-346}$;
those figures are retained as internal diagnostics and deliberately
removed from the headline.

\noindent\textbf{What is open.}\enspace Three questions are load-bearing
and unresolved, stated at the same prominence as the results. Does the
compact geometry exist? The global-analytic layer remains conditional on
a declared hypothesis pack. Where does the gauge sector come from? The
branching chain is compatible with Standard Model matter content but is
not yet a physical breaking mechanism, and this is the largest gap in the
work. Why $(21, 77)$? Five routes to deriving the Betti numbers were
investigated and closed; one explicit residual remains, $\btwo + \bthree
= 98 = \dim(\Kseven) \cdot \dim(\Gtwo)$.

\vspace{0.35em}
\noindent\textbf{The question.}\enspace Most proposals that attempt to
explain the constants of Nature eventually fail. Perhaps this one will
too. But the question does not go away with them: if the constants are
not fundamental, they come from somewhere. This is one attempt to answer
that while making every assumption explicit, every calculation
reproducible, every prediction public, and every route to refutation
available in advance. Whether the hypothesis survives is now a matter for
mathematics and experiment, not for authority.
