{\LARGE\textbf{The Constants of Nature Are Counts}}\\[0.4em]
{\normalsize\itshape A declared correspondence for the Standard Model
parameters, and the measurements that can reject it}\\[0.45em]
{\small Brieuc de La Fourni\`ere \quad | \quad Arithmon program \quad
| \quad \href{https://arithmon.com}{arithmon.com}}
\end{center}

\vspace{-0.3em}
\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.2pt}

{\small

The hypothesis: the dimensionless constants of physics are counts, the
invariants of a geometry, not settings. The $\Kseven$ framework is one
dated answer to it. It assigns 33 closed forms, written in the invariants
of a posited seven-dimensional geometry and of $\E_8 \times \E_8$, to 33
dimensionless parameters of particle physics and cosmology.

It is a candidate, not a derivation. No geometric map is known that sends
the invariants to the observables, and no rule fixes which formula goes
with which observable: the assignments are the author's, and they are
written down as such.

What the framework offers instead of a derivation is a way to be wrong.
An integer ratio cannot be nudged. Each relation is stated as an exact
equality, and it is rejected when the reference data exclude its value at
$3\sigma$. Nothing is added afterwards.

\vspace{0.2em}
\noindent\textbf{What is stated.}
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{0.19\textwidth}p{0.77\textwidth}@{}}
\toprule
The geometry & a compact $\Gtwo$-holonomy seven-manifold with Betti numbers $(\btwo, \bthree) = (21, 77)$, \textbf{posited}: no such manifold is known \\
The dictionary & twenty structural constants and a grammar of formulas \\
The residue register & twelve items, R1 to R12: everything that was set by choice rather than obtained \\
The relations & \textbf{33}, none with a continuously adjustable parameter \\
Formal verification & the arithmetic of the 33 relations is machine-checked in Lean~4, with no unproven step; this certifies the arithmetic, not the physics \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\noindent\textbf{Where the 33 stand.}\enspace Against the most recent
data (PDG 2026, NuFIT 6.1, Planck 2018):
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{0.80\textwidth}r@{}}
\toprule
within $2\sigma$ of measurement & 23 \\
in tension, between $2\sigma$ and $3\sigma$ & 2 \\
excluded as exact equalities at more than $3\sigma$, kept as numerical proximities & 6 \\
inside a PDG range that carries no uncertainty & 1 \\
non-Gaussian profile ($\sin^2\theta_{23}$, in the octant the global fit disfavours) & 1 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
\noindent These are not 33 independent tests. Three sectors are
overdetermined, and their relations disagree with one another as exact
equalities. The other 62 entries of the dataset are conditional
extractions and diagnostics, and are not counted.

\vspace{0.2em}
\noindent\textbf{Is the agreement distinctive? No.}\enspace In a
preregistered test, every competitor searches for its own formulas in the
same grammar and at the same budget. $\Kseven$ reaches 31 of the 33
targets. So do 1\,085 of 4\,159 alternative Betti pairs and 1\,015 of
2\,000 invented vocabularies, and a control with no $\Kseven$ constant
reaches 30. As preregistered, no p-value is reported: closeness to today's
values is not, by itself, evidence. The coincidence probabilities
published with earlier versions are withdrawn.

\vspace{0.2em}
\noindent\textbf{What can reject it.}
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{0.25\textwidth}lp{0.60\textwidth}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Relation} & \textbf{Value} & \textbf{What rejects it} \\
\midrule
$\delta_{\CP}$, the phase $2\pi \cdot 197/360$ & $197^\circ$ & a global fit, including DUNE or Hyper-Kamiokande, whose $3\sigma$ interval excludes $197^\circ$ \\
$\sin^2\theta_{12}$ & $4/13$ & JUNO, whose six-year precision is about four times tighter than the present global fit \\
$\sin^2\theta_{23}$ & $6/11$ & a global fit whose $3\sigma$ interval excludes $6/11$, in either octant \\
$m_\tau/m_e$ & 3477 & a $\tau$-mass measurement that excludes 1776.74 MeV \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
\noindent Reading the integer 197 as an angle in degrees is itself an
assignment of the dictionary. If the $\delta_{\CP}$ relation is rejected,
that assignment goes with it.

\vspace{0.2em}
\noindent\textbf{What is open.}\enspace Does the geometry exist? No
compact $\Gtwo$ manifold with these Betti numbers is known; every ordinary
twisted connected sum is excluded, and one orbifold route is closed. Where
does the gauge sector come from? The Standard-Model algebra sits inside
$\E_8$ through a chain of embeddings, which is not a breaking mechanism;
none is specified. Why $(21, 77)$? The pair is an input: five routes to
deriving it were audited and closed, and one identity, $\btwo + \bthree =
98 = \dim(\Kseven) \cdot \dim(\Gtwo)$, remains as an observation.

\vspace{0.2em}
\noindent\textbf{The question.}\enspace Most proposals that attempt to
explain the constants of Nature eventually fail. Perhaps this one will
too. The question does not go away with them: if the constants are not
fundamental, they come from somewhere. This is one attempt, with its
assumptions listed, its calculations reproducible, and its routes to
rejection stated in advance.
